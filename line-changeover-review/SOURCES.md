# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI from its own published map of manufacturing work, with no third-party text. It applies the long-standing shop-floor practice, widely known as quick changeover, of separating the work that needs a line stopped from the work that can be done while it runs. The clock-time record of the stop, the rule that keeps safety and quality steps exactly as observed, the verb lists that sort each step, the two kinds of idea to shorten, the estimate rule, the test plan and the output format were designed by Fully Powered AI, and the example is invented.
